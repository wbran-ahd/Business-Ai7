# FEATURE_INVENTORY.md — Sauti Baqaya (صوتی بقیہ)

## Features
1. **Voice-Controlled Khata (صوتی منشی):** Speech-to-text entry for income and expenses in Urdu & Roman Urdu.
2. **Customer & Supplier PartyBook (ادھار کھاتہ):** Track customer debts (Udhaar) and supplier payables with phone normalization and WhatsApp receipts.
3. **Horizon Arc & Metric Boxes:** Visual financial dashboards showing cash balance, income, expenses, and net savings.
4. **Monthly Reports & Analytics:** Chart.js visualizations, category breakdowns, and export to CSV/JSON/PDF.
5. **AI Financial Advisor:** Gemini AI integration providing natural language budgeting advice.
6. **Thermal Printing & Sharing:** 58mm/80mm receipt printing and WhatsApp quick sharing links.
7. **Offline PWA Support:** Service worker and localStorage persistence with background Firestore sync.
